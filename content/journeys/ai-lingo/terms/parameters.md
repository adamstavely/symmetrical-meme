## Pronunciation

## Definition

The internal numerical values a model adjusts as it learns. Modern LLMs have billions of parameters, which is why you hear model sizes described as “7 billion” or “70 billion.”

## Usage

The briefing compared a small model with 8 billion parameters against a frontier model many times that size.
