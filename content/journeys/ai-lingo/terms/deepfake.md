# Deepfake / Synthetic Media

## ID

deepfake

## Track

5

## Order

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

DEEP-fayk

## Definition

AI-generated audio, video, or images that convincingly depict real people saying or doing things they never did. A security and media-literacy concern for everyone, not just AI users: voice cloning and fabricated video are now cheap enough to power fraud, impersonation, and disinformation at scale.

## Usage

The finance team added callback verification after a deepfake of the director's voice nearly authorized a wire transfer.
