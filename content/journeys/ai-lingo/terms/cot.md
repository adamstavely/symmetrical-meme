## Definition

Chain-of-thought: prompting or training a model to work through problems step by step rather than jumping to an answer. Reasoning models build this in, spending extra “thinking” time before responding, trading speed and cost for accuracy on hard problems.

## Usage

For the routine lookups they use a fast model, but the policy analysis goes to a reasoning model that works through the logic step by step.
