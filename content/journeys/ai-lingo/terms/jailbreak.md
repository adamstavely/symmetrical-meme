# Jailbreak

## ID

jailbreak

## Track

5

## Order

74

## Part of speech

noun, verb

## Pronunciation

JAYL-brayk

## Definition

A technique for manipulating an AI model into bypassing its own guardrails, typically through cleverly constructed prompts. Distinct from prompt injection: jailbreaking targets the model's rules, injection hijacks it through third-party content.

## Usage

The red team documented three jailbreaks that got the model to produce prohibited content, all patched before launch.
