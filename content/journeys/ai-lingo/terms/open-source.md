# Open Source vs. Closed Source Models

## ID

open-source

## Track

3

## Order

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

OH-pen sors vs. KLOHZD sors

## Definition

The spectrum of model availability. Closed source: accessible only through the provider's service, internals private. Truly open source: weights, code, and training details all public. Most “open” models are actually open weight, somewhere in between.

## Usage

The evaluation compared a closed source model accessed by API against an open source alternative they could host themselves.
