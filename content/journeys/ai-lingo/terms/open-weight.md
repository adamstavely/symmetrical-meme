# Open Weight

## ID

open-weight

## Track

3

## Order

38

## Part of speech

adjective

## Pronunciation

OH-pen WAYT

## Definition

Describing a model whose weights are publicly downloadable, allowing anyone to run, inspect, or fine-tune it on their own infrastructure. Distinct from fully open source, since the training data and code often remain private.

## Usage

They chose an open weight model so sensitive data could stay entirely on agency servers.
