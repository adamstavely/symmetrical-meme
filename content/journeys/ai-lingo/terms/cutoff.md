# Knowledge Cutoff

## ID

cutoff

## Track

2

## Order

26

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

NAH-lij KUT-awf

## Definition

The date after which a model's training data ends. Without live search or retrieval, a model knows nothing about events after its cutoff, no matter how confidently it answers.

## Usage

The model gave an outdated org chart because the reorganization happened after its knowledge cutoff.
