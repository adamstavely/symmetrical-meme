## ID

## Track

## Part of speech

## Pronunciation

## Definition

Systematic unfairness in a model's outputs, usually inherited from patterns in its training data or design choices. Bias can disadvantage groups of people in hiring, lending, and service decisions, which is why AI systems require testing, not trust.

## Usage

The screening tool showed bias against nontraditional resumes, so it was pulled for retraining and review.
