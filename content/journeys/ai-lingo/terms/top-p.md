# Top-P

## ID

top-p

## Track

2

## Order

32

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

tahp-PEE

## Definition

A sampling setting that limits the model's word choices to the smallest set of options whose combined probability reaches a threshold P. Like temperature, it trades consistency against variety; the two are usually tuned together.

## Usage

The vendor recommended adjusting either temperature or top-p, but not both at once.
