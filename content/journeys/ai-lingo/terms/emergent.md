# Emergent Behavior

## ID

emergent

## Track

3

## Order

49

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

ee-MUR-jent bee-HAYV-yur

## Definition

Capabilities that appear in larger models without being explicitly trained, seemingly arising from scale itself. A reminder that model capabilities are discovered as much as designed, which matters for risk assessment.

## Usage

Nobody trained it to do arithmetic; that was emergent behavior that showed up as the models grew.
