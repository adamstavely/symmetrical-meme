## Definition

Personally Identifiable Information: names, ID numbers, contact details, and any data that can identify an individual. The operational rule for AI tools: know where your input goes before you type it, and never enter PII into unapproved tools.

## Usage

The training's first rule was simple: no PII in any AI tool that has not been approved for it.
