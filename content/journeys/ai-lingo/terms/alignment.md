# Model Alignment

## ID

## Track

## Order

## Part of speech

## Pronunciation

MAH-dul uh-LYN-ment

## Definition

The degree to which a model's behavior matches human intentions and values: helpful, honest, and harmless rather than merely capable. Alignment is both a training discipline and an open research problem, and the reason capability alone does not equal trustworthiness.

## Usage

The review asks two separate questions: is the model capable enough, and is its alignment strong enough for this use case?
