# Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

## ID

hitl

## Track

5

## Order

79

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

HYOO-mun in thuh LOOP

## Definition

A design pattern where a human reviews, approves, or can override AI outputs before they take effect. The standard control for consequential decisions, and the answer to “what happens when the model is wrong?”

## Usage

Denials are never automated; the system drafts a recommendation, and a human in the loop makes the call.
