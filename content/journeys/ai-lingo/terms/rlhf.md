# RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

## ID

rlhf

## Track

3

## Order

45

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

ar-el-aych-EF

## Definition

A training stage where humans rank model responses and the model learns to prefer what humans rated highly. RLHF is a major reason modern chatbots feel helpful and polite rather than raw and erratic.

## Usage

The base model was capable but blunt; RLHF is what shaped it into a usable assistant.
