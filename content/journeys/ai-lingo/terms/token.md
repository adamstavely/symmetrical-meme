# Token

## ID

token

## Track

2

## Order

24

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

TOH-ken

## Definition

The unit of text a model actually reads and writes, roughly three-quarters of a word in English. Models measure input, output, cost, and limits in tokens, not words or pages.

## Usage

The API bills per token, so the team trimmed boilerplate from their prompts to cut costs.
