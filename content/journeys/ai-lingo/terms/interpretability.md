# Interpretability / Explainability (XAI)

## ID

interpretability

## Track

5

## Order

78

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

in-tur-prih-tuh-BIL-ih-tee

## Definition

The ability to understand why a model produced a given output. Modern models are largely black boxes, which creates real tension in domains where decisions must be explained to auditors, courts, or the public.

## Usage

For benefits determinations, explainability is a legal requirement, which ruled out the black-box option entirely.
