# Distillation

## ID

distillation

## Track

3

## Order

48

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

dis-tih-LAY-shun

## Definition

Training a smaller, cheaper model to imitate a larger one, transferring much of the capability at a fraction of the size and cost. A common way efficient models are produced.

## Usage

The mobile version was created through distillation, shrinking the flagship model to run on a phone.
