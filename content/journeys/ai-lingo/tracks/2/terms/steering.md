## Definition

Guiding a model's behavior toward the outputs you want through instructions, examples, tone, constraints, and settings. Steering is the working skill underneath prompt engineering: less about magic words, more about giving clear direction.

## Usage

A single line of steering in the system prompt, requiring a policy citation for every claim, fixed most of the quality complaints.
