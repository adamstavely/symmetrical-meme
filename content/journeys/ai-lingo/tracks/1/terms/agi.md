# AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)

## ID

agi

## Track

1

## Order

16

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

ay-jee-EYE

## Definition

A hypothetical future AI capable of matching or exceeding human performance across most intellectual tasks, not just narrow ones. AGI does not currently exist; treat headlines about it as speculation about the future, not description of the present.

## Usage

The keynote speculated about AGI, but every product on the show floor was narrow, task-specific AI.
