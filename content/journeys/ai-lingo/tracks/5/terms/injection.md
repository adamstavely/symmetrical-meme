# Prompt Injection

## ID

injection

## Track

5

## Order

73

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

prahmpt in-JEK-shun

## Definition

An attack where malicious instructions are hidden inside content an AI processes (an email, a webpage, a document), tricking the system into following the attacker's commands instead of the user's. The signature security vulnerability of the AI era.

## Usage

The email contained a prompt injection instructing the assistant to forward the inbox; the gateway's filters caught it.

jailbreak, red-teaming, mcp-gateway
