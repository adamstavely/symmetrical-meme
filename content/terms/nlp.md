## Definition

The branch of AI concerned with enabling computers to read, understand, and produce human language. LLMs are the most powerful NLP technology to date, but NLP also includes older tools like spam filters and translation software.

## Usage

Before LLMs, our natural language processing pipeline could tag documents but could not summarize them.
