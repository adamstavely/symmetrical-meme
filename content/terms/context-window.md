# Context Window

## ID

context-window

## Journey

## Order

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

KAHN-tekst WIN-doh

## Definition

The maximum amount of text (measured in tokens) a model can consider at once: your conversation, any documents you share, and its own responses. Information that falls outside the window is invisible to the model.

## Usage

The contract would not fit in the context window, so she analyzed it section by section.
