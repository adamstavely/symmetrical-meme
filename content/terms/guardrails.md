## Definition

Technical and policy controls that constrain what an AI system will do: topics it declines, actions it cannot take, content filters, and human approval requirements. Guardrails are designed defenses, and like all defenses, they require testing.

## Usage

The deployment includes guardrails that block financial advice and route legal questions to counsel.
