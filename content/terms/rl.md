## Definition

Training a model through trial, error, and reward signals rather than labeled examples: the system learns which behaviors earn rewards and repeats them. The technique behind game-playing AI and a key ingredient in training modern assistants.

## Usage

Reinforcement learning is how the system learned strategy: millions of games against itself, learning from wins and losses.
