## Part of speech

adjective

## Pronunciation

stuh-KAS-tik

## Definition

Involving randomness. LLMs are stochastic by design: they select each next token from a range of probable options rather than always choosing the single most likely one, which is why the same prompt can produce different answers.

## Usage

The output varies between runs because the model is stochastic, not because anything is broken.
