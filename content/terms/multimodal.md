# Multimodal

## ID

multimodal

## Journey

1

## Order

10

## Part of speech

adjective

## Pronunciation

mul-tee-MOH-dul

## Definition

Able to work with more than one type of input or output, such as text, images, audio, and video. A multimodal model can look at a chart and describe it in words.

## Usage

Because the assistant is multimodal, she photographed the whiteboard and asked it to turn the diagram into meeting notes.
