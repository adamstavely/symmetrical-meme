# Temperature

## ID

temperature

## Journey

## Order

31

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

TEM-per-uh-chur

## Definition

A setting that controls how much randomness a model uses when choosing its next token. Low temperature makes output more focused and repeatable; high temperature makes it more varied and creative.

## Usage

They lowered the temperature for the compliance summaries and raised it for the brainstorming assistant.
