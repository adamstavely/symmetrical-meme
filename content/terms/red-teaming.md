# Red Teaming

## ID

red-teaming

## Journey

5

## Order

75

## Part of speech

noun, verb

## Pronunciation

RED TEE-ming

## Definition

Systematically attacking your own AI system before deployment to find failures, vulnerabilities, and harmful behaviors: attempting jailbreaks, injections, and misuse scenarios so adversaries do not find them first.

## Usage

Red teaming is a launch requirement here; no AI system ships until someone has seriously tried to break it.

## Related

jailbreak, injection, governance
