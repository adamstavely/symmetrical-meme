## Definition

The organizational policy defining which AI tools are approved, what data may be used with them, and what uses are prohibited. If you have not read yours, that is the actual first step in AI adoption.

## Usage

Before piloting the new assistant, the team checked the AUP to confirm document uploads were permitted.
