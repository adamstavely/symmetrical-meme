## Pronunciation

## Definition

The full framework of policies, roles, review processes, and controls an organization uses to deploy AI responsibly: who approves what, how risk is assessed, how systems are monitored, and who is accountable when they fail.

## Usage

The AI governance board reviews every proposed use case against a risk tier before anything touches production.
