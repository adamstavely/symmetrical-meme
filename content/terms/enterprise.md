# Enterprise vs. Consumer AI

## ID

enterprise

## Journey

## Order

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

EN-ter-pryz vs. kun-SOO-mer AY-EYE

## Definition

The distinction between AI products governed by an organizational agreement (with data protections, admin controls, and audit capability) and free public tools with no such guarantees. The same brand name can exist in both forms with very different rules.

## Usage

Pasting case notes into the consumer version is prohibited; the enterprise deployment is approved because our data is not used for training.
