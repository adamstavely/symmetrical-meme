## Definition

Zero-shot: asking a model to perform a task with no examples, just instructions. Few-shot: including a handful of examples in your prompt to show the model the pattern you want. Few-shot prompting often dramatically improves consistency.

## Usage

Zero-shot results were hit or miss, but pasting three example entries into the prompt made the formatting perfect every time.
