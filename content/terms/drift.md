# Model Drift

## ID

drift

## Journey

5

## Order

80

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

MAH-dul drift

## Definition

Degradation of a model's real-world performance over time as conditions change from what it was trained on: new terminology, new behaviors, new patterns. The reason deployed AI needs ongoing monitoring, not one-time approval.

## Usage

Accuracy quietly fell eight points over the year; the monitoring dashboard caught the model drift before users did.
