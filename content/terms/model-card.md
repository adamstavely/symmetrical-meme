# Model Card

## ID

model-card

## Journey

3

## Order

40

## Part of speech

noun

## Pronunciation

MAH-dul kard

## Definition

A standardized document describing a model: what it was trained on, what it is intended for, how it was evaluated, and its known limitations. The AI equivalent of a nutrition label, and a reasonable thing to request from any vendor.

## Usage

Ask for the model card before the demo; the limitations section tells you more than the sales deck.
